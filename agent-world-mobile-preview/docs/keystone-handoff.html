What's measured, and what isn't (please read)

  • Build loop (tools/rsi/build_loop.mjs → data/build_ledger.json). Draw calls per frame are counted in headless Chrome with software rendering (SwiftShader). Draw calls don't depend on the GPU. Frame times measured there are not real-GPU frame rates. Every kept change also passed a pixel-diff check against deterministic frames.
  • Real GPU: see the ledger's gpu field for what was measured on Smarth Vader's Mac in this session.
  • Layout search: its score is a model: inverse-square sound, cardioid speakers, delay-tower timing and straight sightlines. It's good for comparing layouts. It does not replace a sound check at a real venue.
  • "Recursive self-improvement" here means a measured improve-and-gate loop over the game's code and the venue layout. The AI model itself does not change.
  • Moon phase: mean-motion estimate, about ±0.5 day. For N3 it gives a last-quarter moon, about 50% lit, rising after the show.
  • Your character only ever quotes your own Show Buddies post. In v1.x the simulation had invented two lines for you; v2 removes them (Rule K3).
  • The Moon Palace island is an artistic model. It is not a survey of the real resort, and it is not affiliated with the resort or with any band.
